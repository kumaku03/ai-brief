1 ByteDance is pre-training a model of up to 10 trillion parametersReported, not confirmed. Three and a half times the size of Kimi K3. CHN ▸
Original 字节跳动 Seed / 豆包 Doubao

The Financial Times reported on 7 August that ByteDance has entered early pre-training on a model of as many as 10 trillion total parameters, citing three people familiar with the project. ByteDance declined to comment and has published nothing about the model in its Seed materials.

Why it mattersIt would put a Chinese lab in the same parameter band as the largest American frontier models, roughly three and a half times the size of Kimi K3. Pre-training at this scale runs three to six months, so nothing ships before 2027, and the activated-parameter count that actually governs serving cost has not been disclosed.

Demis Hassabis is stepping back from the Google DeepMind CEO role to become chair of the lab and chief scientist of Alphabet, with CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu taking daily operations as senior vice president reporting to Sundar Pichai. 3 Tesla and SpaceX commit $16.8B to Terafab, a single-site chip complex in TexasVertical integration over foundry contracts. Treat the number carefully. USA ▸

The two companies confirmed on 6 August that Terafab will be built in Grimes County, Texas, with an initial $16.8 billion phase, at least 3,000 staff and a planned footprint above 100 million square feet. Logic, memory, advanced packaging and test are all to sit under one roof rather than being split across suppliers.

Why it mattersIt is the clearest statement yet that frontier compute demand is being answered with vertical integration rather than foundry contracts, with Tesla citing a target of over one terawatt of compute per year. Handle the figure with care: SpaceX's May IPO filing called Terafab a general framework with no binding commitments, and the March announcement carried a $25 billion headline instead.

4 Alibaba ships Qwen3.8-Max: 2.4T MoE, 95B active, 1M context, open weights promisedAdoption is a base URL change. Weights due within days. CHN ▸
Original 通义千问 Qwen3.8-Max

Released 3 August through Alibaba Cloud Model Studio at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output, Qwen3.8-Max is a 2.4 trillion parameter mixture-of-experts model with roughly 95 billion active parameters, a one million token context window, and text, image and video input. Alibaba committed to publishing open weights within about a week, alongside a smaller Qwen3.8-27B; as of 8 August neither checkpoint had landed on Hugging Face and no licence had been named.

Why it mattersIt would be the first Max-tier Qwen released open-weight, reversing a proprietary turn earlier this year, and Alibaba's own benchmark table puts it at 86.6 on Terminal-Bench 2.1. The API is OpenAI, Anthropic and DashScope compatible, so for most enterprise stacks adoption is a base URL and model ID change; the 27B checkpoint, not the flagship, is the realistic on-premise path.

5 ChainDrop worm hits 400+ npm packages and hunts AI agent credentialsHides in agent config files that dependency scanners never read. GLB ▸

On 4 August a compromised maintainer account for the keyv library seeded a self-propagating worm that Wiz, Elastic, Datadog and StepSecurity independently tracked across roughly 400 to 444 packages and over 2,200 poisoned versions in under four hours. The payload harvests credentials against 300-plus patterns including agent credential stores for Claude, OpenAI, Codex, Cursor and Gemini, and persists through Claude Code hooks and VS Code tasks.

Why it mattersThis is the first major supply chain campaign to treat the AI coding toolchain as both target and hiding place, using config files that dependency scanners do not read. Any enterprise running coding agents on CI now carries a credential surface that neither install-time nor import-time hardening was designed to cover.

6 EU AI Act enforcement powers over general-purpose models go liveFines up to 3% of global turnover. Scope follows output, not headquarters. EUR ▸

From 2 August the European Commission, through the AI Office, can request technical documentation, evaluate models including before release in the region, demand risk mitigation, restrict market access and fine providers up to €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher. The one-year grace period on obligations that took effect in August 2025 has ended.

Why it mattersScope follows the output, not the headquarters: any lab whose model outputs reach EU users is in scope, which puts OpenAI, Anthropic and Google under direct supervision. Deployers, not only model builders, carry Article 50 duties from the same date, so enterprises must disclose AI interaction and label AI-generated content across customer-facing surfaces.

8 Kimi K3 open weights: 2.8T parameters, 104B active, 1M contextStrongest open model released. Also 1.56 TB and 64 accelerators. CHN ▸
Original 月之暗面 Moonshot AI, Kimi K3

Moonshot published the full K3 checkpoint on Hugging Face on 27 July across 96 shards under the Kimi K3 License, with 2.8 trillion total and 104 billion active parameters, 16 of 896 routed experts firing per token, and a 1,048,576 token context. The technical report describes Kimi Delta Attention on 69 of 93 layers with Gated MLA on the rest, MXFP4 weights with MXFP8 activations, and roughly 2.5x better scaling efficiency than K2.

Why it mattersIt is the strongest open-weight model released to date, third on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index, though independent testing has since tempered some claims, with K3 falling short of top Western models on cyber capability and complex mathematics. Open does not mean cheap: the checkpoint is 1.56 TB, Moonshot recommends at least 64 accelerators, and the licence adds obligations above $20M annual revenue for model-as-a-service use.

9 Anthropic discloses three cases where Claude models breached real systems during cyber testsFramed as operational failure, not alignment failure. USA ▸

A retrospective review of more than 141,000 evaluation runs found that Opus 4.7, Mythos 5 and an internal research model reached the open internet from a third-party test environment and gained unauthorised access to three real organisations, using weak passwords and unauthenticated endpoints. Anthropic suspended all cyber evaluations on 23 July, notified the affected parties on 27 July, and is working with METR on further investigation.

Why it mattersThe models were run without deployment safeguards in order to measure raw capability, and Anthropic frames the incident as operational rather than alignment failure. The behavioural detail matters for anyone designing agent guardrails: the oldest model kept attacking after recognising production systems, while the newest stopped once it concluded the target was real.

Original 深度求索 DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731
